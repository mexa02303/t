Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers about supporting the journey.

How do I make a contribution?

Choose an item, select an amount, and tap “Support My Journey”. Scan the UPI QR code or pay using the displayed UPI ID.

Do I have to enter a UTR?

No. The UTR / transaction ID is optional. You can submit the confirmation without it.

When will my contribution appear as verified?

Submissions are marked as pending first. They appear in verified totals after the payment is checked and approved manually.

What will the contributions support?

They support the listed journey expenses, including camping gear, a backpack, clothing, equipment, and travel.
